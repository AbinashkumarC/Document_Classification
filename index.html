Under the Hood
How Classification Works

A multi-stage pipeline transforms your raw PDF into structured document labels in seconds.

01

PDF Upload

The PDF is securely uploaded and saved temporarily on the server for processing.

02

OCR Extraction

Tesseract OCR converts each page image (200 DPI) to machine-readable text, even for scanned documents.

03

Smart Sampling

For multi-page files, the top 25% of the first 6 pages and last page are extracted to reduce processing time.

04

Fuzzy Keyword Match

RapidFuzz partial-ratio matching (≥90% threshold) identifies document types — even with OCR noise.

05

Regex Rules

Aadhaar number patterns, PAN card patterns, and implant sticker heuristics run in parallel.

06

Result + Logging

Classifications are returned instantly and logged to PostgreSQL for auditing and analytics.

Real-World Impact
Who Benefits & How

DocScan AI bridges the gap between paper-heavy healthcare operations and digital efficiency.

🛡️

Insurance Industry

Health insurance companies process thousands of claim documents daily. Manually sorting discharge summaries, KYC forms, pre-auth requests, and bills is time-consuming and error-prone. DocScan AI automates this entirely.

  • Auto-sort incoming claim document bundles in milliseconds
  • Flag missing mandatory documents (Discharge Summary, Pre-Auth, Bill) before claim adjudication
  • Detect fraudulent or duplicate Aadhaar / PAN submissions
  • Accelerate cashless claim turnaround from hours to minutes
  • Generate audit trails for IRDAI compliance requirements
  • Reduce claim rejection rates caused by misclassified documents
  • Integrate with TPA (Third Party Administrator) workflows for real-time routing
🏥

Hospital & Healthcare

Hospitals generate enormous amounts of paperwork per patient admission — prescriptions, lab reports, OT notes, consent forms, radiology reports. Organizing these for discharge or insurance submission is a major bottleneck.

  • Automatically build patient digital record by classifying uploaded scans
  • Ensure all mandatory discharge documents are present before patient release
  • Separate billing documents (Pharmacy, OT, Room, Ambulance) for accurate invoicing
  • Speed up insurance pre-authorization by identifying required docs instantly
  • Support NABH-accreditation-ready documentation workflows
  • Help medical records department manage legacy paper archives digitally
  • Reduce patient waiting time at billing and discharge counters
